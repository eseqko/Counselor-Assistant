Template columns:
  • Student ID # (required, must match caseload)
  • Date (required, MM/DD/YYYY)
  • Period (0-10, optional — 1-4 core, 5 extracurricular, 6 advisory)
  • Status (required: Present, Absent, Tardy, Excused)
  • Course Name (optional)
  • Reason (optional)
Step 2: Upload Data
What happens on upload:
  • Records are matched to students by Student ID # / Perm ID
  • Duplicate entries (same student+date+period) are skipped
  • Invalid rows are reported
Synergy SIS compatible:
  • Upload the Synergy attendance report directly — no reformatting needed
  • The format is auto-detected (looks for Period 0, Period 1, … columns)
  • Only students on your caseload are imported; the rest are skipped
  • Synergy codes (Illness, Cut, Activity, etc.) are mapped automatically
