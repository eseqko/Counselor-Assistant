Template columns:

  • Last Name (required)
  • Grade (6-12, required)
  • Student ID # (required, unique)
  • Email (optional)
  • EL Status (Newcomer, LTEL, RFEP, or EO)
  • EL Level (EL 1, EL 2, EL 3 - only for Newcomers)
  • IEP (Yes or blank)
  • 504 Plan (Yes or blank)
Step 2: Upload & Preview
New school year? Upload your new roster and click Preview Changes — you'll see who's returning, who's new, and who from your current caseload is missing from the file, and can choose what happens to each departing student before anything is saved.
  • New Student IDs are added to your caseload
  • Existing Student IDs are updated (incl. new grade level)
  • Students missing from the file are kept unless you choose otherwise
